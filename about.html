OUR JOURNEY

Built from a real problem. Trusted in production for years.

It began with the opportunity to meet a pressing need. IPVision (now Dstny A/S) had a thriving business but urgently required a modern, reliable, scalable solution to manage its entire telecom operations. Leveraging extensive telecom expertise, a unique solution was developed — and evolved into ATSuite, a CPQ and Billing platform ideally suited for subscription-based B2B businesses. Six-plus years in production as a managed service later, today we help companies streamline and grow with our platform.
