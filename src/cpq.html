INTEGRATIONS · CRM

Quote from the CRM your team already lives in.

ATSuite CPQ plugs bi-directionally into the leading CRMs — accounts, contacts and opportunities stay in sync, while configured quotes, approvals and signed orders flow back to the deal record automatically. Reps stay in their CRM; finance gets clean, structured data downstream.

  • Two-way sync of accounts, contacts and opportunities — no duplicate entry
  • Generate ATSuite quotes directly from a CRM opportunity record
  • Closed-won deals hand off to subscription & billing — no rekeying
  • Pre-built connectors plus an open REST API for custom CRMs
