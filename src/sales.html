INTEGRATIONS · CRM

Works with the CRM your team already uses.

ATSuite connects bi-directionally to leading CRMs — accounts, contacts and opportunities stay in sync, while quotes, approvals and orders flow back automatically. Reps keep working in the tool they know; finance and operations get the structured data they need downstream.

  • ✓Two-way sync of accounts, contacts and opportunities — no duplicate data entry.
  • ✓Quotes generated in ATSuite appear on the CRM opportunity record with status and amount.
  • ✓Closed-won deals trigger subscription, billing and provisioning workflows automatically.
  • ✓Pre-built connectors plus an open REST API for custom or in-house CRM systems.
